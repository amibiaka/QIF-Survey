Purpose

This platform supports the official Survey on Financing Quality Infrastructure and MSME Access to Finance, conducted by UNIDO under the ACP Quality Infrastructure Programme, in collaboration with the OACPS, AUDA-NEPAD, the African Union Commission, CROSQ and the Pacific Islands Forum Secretariat. It is intended for invited respondents and authorised administrators.

Access and accounts

Respondent links and administrator credentials are personal and non-transferable; do not share them. Attempting to access data you are not authorised to see, probing or scanning the service, scraping content, or interfering with its operation is prohibited.

Acceptable use

Use the platform only to take part in, or administer, the survey. Provide accurate information; where you are not sure of an answer, the questionnaire lets you say so rather than guess.

Content

The survey instruments, texts and analyses are the property of the implementing partners. Do not reproduce them outside the survey without permission.

Service

The platform is provided in good faith, as is. The partners may suspend or modify it at any time for maintenance or security reasons.

Reporting a problem

Report vulnerabilities or misuse to idriss.amine@gmail.com. A machine-readable contact is published at /.well-known/security.txt.
